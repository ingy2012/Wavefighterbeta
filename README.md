 🌊 Wavefighter

🎮 Control smart — before you even start: tap “Enter Arena” on phone → touchscreen; press A on controller → gamepad; press Enter → keyboard. (Remap in settings!)

⚔️ 3 styles, 9+ weapons

    Shooter — dual pistol (no zoom), shotgun, assault rifle (1x), sniper (2x zoom).
    Soldier — short sword + shield, 2‑handed claymore (slow & strong), spear (long reach).
    Magician — Right hand: Fire blast (AoE), Ice Spike (freeze/slow), Lightning (falloff). Left hand: Teleport, Argis shield (invincible), Poison Spike (poison/DoT), Air Burst.

🎯 3 shooting modes

    Unlimited — no ammo, no score, just climb levels.
    Ammo + Reload — limited ammo, reload each level.
    Precision score — unlimited ammo, but score multiplier drops the more you shoot: be precise.
